# DIRE BET terms — draft for legal review

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION.** Prepared 2026-11-20 for a production-configured free-play service. A qualified Ethiopian lawyer and Telegram must review this document before publication.

## Current service status

DIRE BET offers free practice and free online rooms while its real-money activation gate is off. Deposits, withdrawals and paid rooms are unavailable unless the required legal, licensing, platform and payment-provider approvals are recorded and the owner activates the service. No payment-provider API is connected: if activated, Telebirr and eBirr deposits are reviewed manually from submitted references and screenshots; withdrawals are sent manually and marked complete with a transfer reference. These workflows are not an offer to accept funds while the gate is closed. Ethiopian 41 is unavailable.

## Eligibility and safe play

DIRE BET is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Free play does not currently require an age review. Real-money features require an approved 18+ age-eligibility check and remain unavailable until the owner has selected a suitable age-check method, recorded all required approvals and obtained qualified legal confirmation. The service must not offer real-money features unless all required approvals and responsible-play controls are in place. Users should stop if play causes distress or loss of control. Self-exclusion, cooling-off, limits and support contact are not yet available as complete player-facing controls.

## Accounts, conduct and service availability

Telegram profile information may be used to create an account after Telegram init-data verification. Users must not impersonate others or interfere with the service. Users can sign out from the Account screen. Availability, multiplayer and account tools may change.

## Support, disputes and changes

Support contact details and a full complaints process have not yet been configured. Do not use this draft to resolve payment or prize disputes. The final version must explain any activated payment and prize terms, complaints, account closure, liability, governing law and data rights with counsel’s approval.
